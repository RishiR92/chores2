# Asmi AI-to-AI insight film — shorter proof, bigger reveal

## Core idea

Rebuild the film around a discovery Asmi is seeing in the real world, not around the restaurant task itself:

**Thousands already use Asmi to deal with the real world. Increasingly, the voice answering Asmi is also AI. Here is the proof.**

The Herman’s call becomes one short, undeniable piece of evidence. The recording ends exactly after **“Herman’s is fully vegan.”** The rest of the film plays in complete silence so the insight lands with weight.

## Story and proposed copy

### 1. Establish authority — 0:00–0:04

Open at scale, with many live connections forming around Asmi rather than one decorative dot.

Copy:

- `thousands of people use asmi`
- `to deal with the real world.`

### 2. Introduce the observation — 0:04–0:08

The network changes: some answering points visibly identify as AI. No fake percentage or fabricated chart.

Copy:

- `lately, we’ve noticed something.`
- `more calls are being answered by AI.`

This is the premise, presented as an insight from Asmi’s real activity.

### 3. Show the proof — 0:08–0:23

Select one call from the larger network and mark it clearly:

- `ONE REAL ASMI CALL`
- `ASMI · AI CALLER`
- `HERMAN’S · AI HOST`

Use only the clean chronological exchange:

1. Herman’s AI: `“Yes, of course. I can help you with a booking, cancellation, or any questions about Herman’s. What can I do for John today?”`
2. Asmi: `“John is looking to visit on August 21st and wanted to check what vegetarian menu options you’ll have available that day.”`
3. Herman’s AI: `“Just to clarify, Herman’s is fully vegan.”`

End the call audio precisely after **“vegan.”** Remove the plant-based continuation and the entire rotating-buffet section.

Captions remain large and exact. The responding node identifies itself as AI only after the audience hears it speak, turning the call into proof rather than an explanation.

### 4. Let the insight land — 0:23–0:31

At the final word, all music and call audio stop. Hold a brief beat of pure silence, then pull back to reveal this call among many AI-answering signals.

Copy, one thought at a time:

- `an AI asked.`
- `an AI answered.`
- `the task kept moving.`
- `this is already happening.`

The sequence should feel like a discovery becoming obvious, not a feature list.

### 5. Name the shift — 0:31–0:36

The wider network now reads as a new operating layer for the real world.

Copy:

- `the real world is becoming agent-to-agent.`
- `asmi is already there.`

No music returns. Silence is part of the reveal.

### 6. Final screen — 0:36–0:39

Only the real Asmi wordmark and one concise line:

- `built for whoever answers.`

No extra explanation, CTA, icons, or overlapping copy.

## Visual direction

- Keep the dark signal-world concept, but make the network communicate scale and change—not empty atmosphere.
- Begin with human/business endpoints; progressively reveal more endpoints as AI responders without inventing metrics.
- Treat the selected Herman’s conversation as a magnified real event inside the larger system.
- Make typography carry the argument: large, decisive statements with clear pauses and strong hierarchy.
- Use continuous spatial movement from network → evidence → wider network; no slides, cards, dashboards, or generic data graphics.
- Widescreen and vertical remain separately composed for readable feed-size copy.

## Audio direction

- Shorten the call to the three proof beats above.
- Cut the final excerpt immediately after the complete phrase `Herman’s is fully vegan.` with a natural micro-tail and no clipped consonants.
- Use music only through the proof section, building restrained momentum beneath the call.
- Duck the score under speech, then remove it completely on the word `vegan`.
- Keep every insight and final-brand frame silent—no score, sting, ambience, or dialogue spill.
- Master both versions for clear phone playback at approximately -14 LUFS and a safe true peak.
