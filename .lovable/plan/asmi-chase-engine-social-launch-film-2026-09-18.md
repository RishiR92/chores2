# Asmi Chase Engine — social launch film

## Product verdict

Build a new **vertical-first social film around one complete real-world chase**, not another broad Asmi montage.

The clearest story is the existing **broken AC + ghosting landlord** scenario:

- instantly relatable and emotionally urgent
- complex enough to require multiple people and channels
- naturally proves persistence over days, not just a quick phone call
- already supported by Asmi’s current story language: **3 days, 11 messages, Thursday 10am, work order #4471**
- can reuse the existing HVAC call material where it truthfully fits

The audience should understand within five seconds:

> one message to Asmi starts a real-world chase that keeps running across calls, texts, and email until there is a confirmed outcome.

## Story — 30 seconds

### 1. Hook: the problem follows you — 0:00–0:03

Open on a hot apartment at night: still ceiling fan, thermostat reading high, phone lighting the room.

A native message lands:

`my AC is dead. my landlord's ghosting me.`

Asmi answers:

`on it. go sleep.`

The send bubble becomes the motion trigger for the entire film.

### 2. Define the product through action — 0:03–0:06

As the phone moves into the chase, use one sharp statement—not a diagram:

`Asmi is a chase engine for the real world.`

Then the promise appears as the action begins:

`calls. texts. emails. however long it takes.`

Keep these lines separate from the phone so neither obscures the native interface.

### 3. The first attempts — 0:06–0:11

Show realistic, causally connected communication:

1. **CALL — landlord:** rings, then voicemail.
2. The red call control travels around the phone edge and becomes a blue send bubble.
3. **TEXT — landlord:** `AC is out. Need access approval today.`
4. Read receipt appears. No reply.

The same pursuit line remains alive between attempts so viewers feel one continuing job, not separate scenes.

### 4. Escalation — 0:11–0:18

The unread text physically unfolds into a real Mail compose surface:

- **To:** property manager / maintenance
- **Subject:** `Urgent AC repair — work order request`
- essential details only: address, access window, request for written confirmation

The send arrow races into a phone call to an HVAC provider. Use a short, intelligible excerpt from the existing HVAC call audio where the dialogue matches the booking; do not add a fake transcript.

A restrained time ribbon advances behind the phone:

`11:18 AM` → `DAY 2` → `DAY 3`

Attempts accumulate as tactile evidence around the device:

`CALL 03` · `TEXT 06` · `EMAIL 02`

This is how the film proves **minutes or days** without stopping the story for an explanation.

### 5. The turn — 0:18–0:23

An incoming call connects. The green connected state pushes through the glass into a native confirmation:

`Thursday · 10:00 AM`

`Work order #4471`

`Technician confirmed`

Then reveal the full pursuit receipt:

`3 days. 11 touchpoints. one message from you.`

### 6. Product payoff — 0:23–0:27

The 11 attempt markers collapse into one bold result stamp:

`DONE.`

Then land the core value in two beats:

`she tries every channel.`

`she doesn't stop until it's done.`

No language montage, task carousel, or achievement statistics in this film—the single story is the proof.

### 7. Brand resolve — 0:27–0:30

Resolve into the real Asmi logo on warm paper with the approved Messages and WhatsApp marks.

Final copy:

`text asmi.`

`give her the thing you've been avoiding.`

End on one dry physical click, with enough hold for the logo and entry points to register.

## Creative direction

### Visual world

Preserve the approved V9/V11 identity:

- warm paper, ink, electric blue, coral, citrus, and mint
- Bricolage Grotesque for campaign type, native system type inside the phone, Space Mono for timestamps and evidence
- tactile print texture, crisp shadows, torn edges, phone glass, and decisive color changes
- realistic full-height iPhone with readable Phone, Messages, and Mail surfaces

Add a restrained real-world layer specific to this story: heat shimmer, thermostat details, maintenance labels, work-order paper, and changing ambient light to show time passing. Keep it graphic and premium, not stock-footage literal.

### Signature motion system

- **One uninterrupted pursuit:** every failed attempt physically creates the next channel.
- **Persistent chase line:** a single animated line travels behind and around the phone, changing color with each channel and never resetting until confirmation.
- **Time compression:** daylight, timestamps, and attempt counters advance around the stable phone instead of using calendar slides.
- **Escalation:** movements become faster and tighter through voicemail → ignored text → email → provider call.
- **Resolution:** all motion and music stop briefly before the confirmed appointment lands.

Avoid cards explaining the channels, generic wipes, progress diagrams, floating icons, repetitive centered scenes, confetti, fake app chrome, or text covering the phone.

## Sound and pacing

Use the approved energetic V9 launch track as the baseline, recut to the 30-second arc rather than replacing its personality.

- immediate pulse under the first message
- rising gear shifts for call → text → email → provider call
- short, truthful excerpt from the existing HVAC call recording, with music ducked underneath
- native ring, voicemail, send, mail, vibration, and connection sounds kept crisp but secondary
- brief silence before the confirmed outcome
- euphoric return for the final promise, then a dry logo click

Final master: 48 kHz stereo, approximately -14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1 dBTP.

## Deliverables

- New versioned composition; preserve V9 and V11 unchanged
- Primary social master: **1080×1920, 30 seconds, 30 fps, H.264/AAC**
- Widescreen adaptation: **1920×1080**, separately composed rather than cropped
- Final MP4s plus key-frame contact sheets for review

## PM validation

The film passes only if a sound-off viewer can answer all five questions after one watch:

1. What did the user ask Asmi to do?
2. What failed first?
3. Which channels did Asmi try?
4. Did the work continue over multiple days without the user managing it?
5. What concrete real-world result was confirmed?

Do not add broader feature claims unless they strengthen one of those answers.

## Top-designer verification

1. Review a silent animatic before final sound; the chase must read without captions or narration.
2. Render the first six seconds separately; reject it unless problem, product, and value are immediately clear.
3. Inspect every Phone, Messages, and Mail frame at playback speed on a phone-sized display.
4. Confirm essential text stays outside social-platform top and bottom overlays.
5. Reject any frame that resembles a presentation slide, task dashboard, generic app promo, or motion template.
6. Verify that each channel transition is story-specific and visibly caused by the failed attempt before it.
7. Confirm the elapsed-time device clearly communicates `minutes or days` without slowing the film.
8. Check that the call recording matches the visible HVAC moment and remains intelligible over music.
9. Review vertical first, then redesign the widescreen framing shot by shot.
10. Verify duration, frame rate, codecs, audio rate, loudness, peak safety, and both final exports before delivery.
