# AI-to-AI film — V5 suspense-first rebuild

## Creative reset

This is not an explainer about AI-to-AI. It is a reveal film: the viewer first notices something strange inside an ordinary Asmi task, hears the proof, then understands the larger shift and why Asmi is ahead of it.

**Direction:** premium consumer-tech thriller that breaks into a bright, kinetic launch film after the reveal.

- **Before the reveal:** restrained, close, curious, slightly playful. Macro details of a live call, clipped typography, timecode, waveform, and withheld identity. No nodes, networks, AI labels, dots, or language that gives away who answered.
- **At the reveal:** the final words “Herman’s is fully vegan” trigger the visual and musical release. The call surface physically splits open and exposes the two sides: Asmi’s agent and the restaurant’s agent.
- **After the reveal:** concrete evidence, not abstract particles. Real call records, answered-by-agent markers, consumer requests, business responses, and completed outcomes move through one connected system.

## Story and exact copy

### 1. The pattern — 0:00–0:05

A rapid but controlled stream of real-world task fragments moves through a dark editorial space: restaurant, dentist, gym, landlord, salon. We never show who is on the other end.

Copy builds as one continuous thought, not separate title cards:

> asmi makes thousands of calls into the real world.
>
> lately, something has changed on the other end.
>
> listen.

The camera keeps pushing toward one selected call. Music is a tense, syncopated pulse with a rising unanswered phrase.

### 2. The proof — 0:05–0:16

One uninterrupted Herman’s call becomes the whole frame. The three clean audio excerpts play chronologically and end exactly on:

> “Just to clarify, Herman’s is fully vegan.”

Visuals resemble a live call artifact, not a fake transcript: Asmi’s request is shown as an active consumer intent, the restaurant response appears as captured call evidence, and the remote identity remains deliberately unresolved. Captions follow the recording exactly. The score ducks but never disappears.

### 3. The reveal — 0:16–0:20

On “vegan,” the waveform freezes for a fraction of a beat, then the call artifact unfolds into two labeled sides:

> ASMI AGENT
>
> BUSINESS AGENT

A single line stamps across the connection:

> an AI just called an AI.

This is the only explicit AI-to-AI reveal, saved until after the audience has heard the proof. The score releases into its main beat here.

### 4. The insight — 0:20–0:28

The revealed call is pulled upward into a growing stack of recognizable call records. Each record carries useful evidence: task type, business category, “agent answered,” and outcome. Records arrive as receipts, switchboard strips, and call-history rows—not floating dots or meaningless lines.

Copy stays anchored while the evidence accumulates around it:

> more real-world calls are being answered by agents.
>
> agent-to-agent isn’t coming.
>
> it’s already happening.

The second and third lines join the first in one accumulating composition rather than replacing it slide by slide.

### 5. Why Asmi matters — 0:28–0:35

The call records transform into a fast consumer-side task board. Five clear requests enter from people:

- book a dentist
- cancel a gym membership
- book a gardener
- chase a landlord
- move a salon appointment

Each request passes through Asmi, meets the business side—human or agent—and returns as a concrete result. The system stays visually legible: **you → Asmi → the real world → done**.

Copy:

> businesses have agents answering.
>
> consumers have asmi calling.

Then the whole board resolves into:

> asmi is leading the consumer side of the agent-to-agent world.

### 6. End — 0:35–0:39

The task board folds into the real Asmi wordmark. One final line lands with the music’s resolved hit:

> your agent for the real world.

No extra paragraph, no competing labels, no clutter.

## Visual system

### Palette

A reveal-driven palette rather than the V4 green-dot world:

- **Ink:** `#11110F` — suspense background and type
- **Paper:** `#F4F1E8` — call records and evidence surfaces
- **Cobalt:** `#2457FF` — Asmi / consumer side
- **Acid:** `#C8FF45` — reveal and completed outcomes
- **Coral:** `#FF625C` — business-side markers and tension accents

Before the reveal, cobalt/acid/coral are mostly withheld. The reveal unlocks the full palette, making the aha moment visible even without sound.

### Typography

- **Display:** Archivo Black — compact, high-impact reveal and end frame
- **Body/evidence:** Instrument Sans — crisp call records, captions, and task details
- Maximum two fonts. Large feed-safe copy with strict safe areas in both formats.

### Motion system

- **Default entrance:** objects enter through masked mechanical pulls, like call records advancing through a switchboard.
- **Default exit:** the same object is physically captured by the next scene and transformed; no fades to black and no unrelated wipes.
- **Suspense camera:** slow macro push with shallow parallax and tiny waveform movement.
- **Reveal accent:** freeze → snap-open → color release → rapid depth pullback.
- **Post-reveal rhythm:** call records stamp, stack, and route into completed consumer tasks on the main beat.
- **Transitions:** one continuous object transformation from call artifact → evidence stack → task board → logo.

### Backgrounds and effects

- Remove the magnetic dot field entirely.
- Use tactile paper grain, cropped call metadata, waveform slices, timecode, and moving evidence strips as functional backgrounds.
- Background information clears away from every headline and remains lower contrast than the primary message.
- No generic neural network, node lattice, glowing sci-fi interface, or decorative lines.

## Music and sound design

Create a new 39-second instrumental built specifically around the reveal:

1. **0:00–0:05:** muted tick, elastic bass pulse, rising unresolved motif.
2. **0:05–0:16:** minimal tension bed under the call; voice remains clean and forward.
3. **Final “vegan”:** short hold, impact, then the full groove enters—bright percussion, bass, and a confident melodic hook.
4. **0:20–0:35:** energetic consumer-launch rhythm synchronized to record stamps and completed-task hits.
5. **0:35–0:39:** clean final lift and resolved logo chord.

The music continues throughout, ducks under speech, and never introduces an unrelated melody or random beat drop.

## Verification

- **Spoiler test:** no AI-to-AI wording, labels, paired agents, or network imagery appears before the final call line.
- **Aha test:** the identity reveal is unmistakable with sound and still understandable muted.
- **Insight test:** post-call visuals explain the rise in agent-answered calls and Asmi’s consumer-side role without unsupported numbers.
- **Story test:** every transition carries the same call artifact forward; no slide-deck resets.
- **Feed test:** captions and key copy remain readable at social-feed size in widescreen and vertical.
- **Collision test:** inspect contact sheets and key frames for background/type overlap.
- **Audio test:** listen through both masters, checking exact call boundaries, ducking, reveal impact, continuity, loudness, and ending.
