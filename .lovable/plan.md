# Asmi launch film V8 - cinematic product film reset

## Creative-director verdict on V7

V7 should not be repaired scene by scene. Its visual grammar is the problem.

- **The picture still behaves like slides.** Nine self-contained scenes fade or scale into the same dark void. The channel carousel, task cloud, phone, proof, numbers, and logo all reset instead of one action causing the next.
- **The channel explanation is still a diagram.** Rounded cards labelled call, text, email, and call again tell the viewer what happened instead of letting them experience the escalation.
- **The task-selection moment is generic.** Small notes orbiting in empty space feel like a feature cloud. Nothing has weight, urgency, or a believable relationship to the user.
- **The device lacks hero-product presence.** The current phone is a styled flat frame, not a photographed or rendered object with convincing titanium, glass, edge depth, reflections, lens perspective, and native screen scale.
- **The visual language turns playful into cartoonish.** Emoji-like symbols, glowing dots, colored confetti, rounded tiles, and constant spring motion weaken the credibility of the product.
- **The achievements are title cards.** `20,000` and `30M` are readable, but the dots behind them look decorative rather than like evidence of real work and real attention.
- **The music is loud but emotionally flat.** It has no true silence across 30 seconds, remains almost equally dense through every scene, and leaves no headroom for transitions or proof moments. The jazz-house brass and continuous groove make the film feel novelty-driven rather than premium.
- **The plan and execution diverged.** V7 promised one continuous camera move, object-to-object transitions, and realistic interfaces. The result still uses independent compositions, captions, a progress rail, and centered layouts.

## New direction: one message starts a real-world chain reaction

Create a **premium cinematic product film**, not a motion-graphics explainer. The reference level is a major consumer-tech launch: product tactility, real-world consequence, disciplined type, large image scale, and a score built around tension and release.

The film lives in one coherent photographed-feeling world. A real-looking iPhone and its screen are the hero. Messages, call states, email, documents, and confirmations inherit motion from one another. There are no channel cards, task clouds, progress diagrams, confetti, emoji icons, glowing pursuit dot, or generic black title-card resets.

## 2026 quality bar: bespoke, not template motion

The entire V7 creative system is rejected, not reused with nicer styling. V8 will not inherit its animations, compositions, decorative devices, transition timing, typography treatment, or score logic.

- **Every shot needs a visual idea.** No content floating over a background. Each frame must reveal material, scale, depth, light, or cause-and-effect.
- **Transitions are authored around the story.** The call button becoming Messages, the sent reply becoming an email subject, and the mail arrow becoming the connected call are three different physical transformations, not one reusable wipe preset.
- **The film must feel photographed.** Use cinematic focal-length changes, foreground occlusion, parallax, reflections, controlled motion blur, depth falloff, and motivated lighting. Avoid the weightless feel of flat vector layers.
- **Graphics behave like title design, not app presentation.** Type may be cropped by the frame, revealed by objects, reflected in glass, or discovered through focus. It never arrives as a centered headline plus icons.
- **Product UI stays credible.** When the screen is readable, the camera is stable and the interface is native. When the camera becomes expressive, the copy is no longer asking to be read.
- **Premium restraint replaces cartoon energy.** Remove glowing dots, bouncing springs, orbiting notes, colored shockwaves, emoji marks, confetti, playful scatter, and decorative motion without narrative purpose.
- **Originality comes from one proprietary move:** Asmi's persistence is expressed by communication controls physically transforming across the same device while the camera continues one uninterrupted pursuit. This becomes the film's recognizable signature, not a borrowed trend.
- **Review standard:** any frame that could pass as a SaaS explainer slide, app-store animation, motion template, or mobile-game reward screen is rejected before the full render.

### Visual language

- Near-black physical studio environment with motivated practical light: phone glow, reflected blue glass, restrained warm key light, and one red friction accent.
- A highly realistic recent-iPhone hero with correct tall proportions, metal rails, glass depth, buttons, Dynamic Island, speaker/camera detail, real reflections, and believable camera perspective.
- Realistic native Phone, Messages, and Mail surfaces composited into the device at readable scale.
- One camera grammar: macro push-ins, 3/4 product orbits, rack-focus, edge passes, and match cuts generated by visible interface details.
- One restrained grotesque family for campaign copy and native system type inside the phone. The supplied Asmi wordmark appears only at the end.
- Large subjects and decisive framing. No small objects floating in empty space and no repeated centered compositions.

## Story and edit - approximately 32 seconds

### 1. Brand provocation - 0:00-0:02.5

Start almost silent on an extreme macro of the iPhone edge and vibrating screen light. The camera catches only fragments as the line resolves in three sharp cuts:

`the most irritating`

`assistant.`

`for them.`

The final period is not a cartoon puck. It becomes the red live-call indicator already present on the phone screen, pulling the viewer directly into action.

### 2. Breadth without a task cloud - 0:02.5-0:05.5

The camera tracks past a rapid physical corridor of real completed-task artifacts at different depths: a boarding pass changing gates, a dentist appointment confirmation, an insurance approval letter, a restaurant reservation, a plumber arrival window, a refunded charge, a passport reminder, and a vet booking.

Each artifact can carry one restrained, object-specific pictogram for faster recognition: a salon chair for a haircut, aircraft for a flight, tooth for a dentist, wrench for a plumber, fork and glass for dinner, passport mark for renewal, paw for a vet, and shield/document for insurance. These are premium monochrome marks integrated into the printed or screen artifact, not colorful app icons, floating stickers, or a recreated website grid.

These are not presented for selection. They are glimpses of Asmi already working in the world. The icon helps the category register instantly while the artifact supplies realism. Focus lands on one incoming iMessage:

`they won't let me cancel my gym. i've tried twice.`

### 3. One-message handoff - 0:05.5-0:08

The real iPhone settles square and fills the frame. The short exchange is immediately readable:

- send me your member ID.
- GX-88214
- got it. don't think about it again.

The sent bubble travels under the glass and becomes the live call screen. This is the moment the audience understands that one message started the work.

### 4. Multichannel pursuit - 0:08-0:18

Show the escalation entirely through one phone and one continuous camera move. No pre-explainer, channel carousel, labels, progress rail, or captions.

1. **Call:** Mission Gym rings, reaches voicemail. The voicemail state visibly rejects the attempt.
2. **Text:** The red end-call control glides around the physical edge of the phone and becomes the blue Messages send control. The gym replies that cancellation must be in person. Asmi answers with the written-notice clause.
3. **Email:** The final blue reply stretches into the Mail subject line. The camera follows it into a realistic compose sheet showing `Cancellation notice - GX-88214`, section 4.2, and the $59 refund request.
4. **Call again:** The send arrow exits the screen, travels across the titanium edge as reflected light, and becomes the second incoming call indicator. It connects green.

Only after the viewer has watched this causal chain does one compact line appear in the environment beside the phone:

`she tries every way in until it's done.`

### 5. Outcome - 0:18-0:20.5

The connected-call green expands through the glass and reveals the confirmation in the user's Messages thread rather than as a detached card:

`Your membership has been cancelled.`

`$59 refunded`

`GX-88214`

Hold long enough to read. The phone gives one restrained confirmation vibration. No decorative effects.

### 6. Breadth payoff - 0:20.5-0:23

The same interface-to-interface transformation repeats in accelerated glimpses across three other real tasks from the opening corridor: flight rebooked, dentist confirmed, insurance claim approved. This proves the gym story is one example of a repeatable behavior without returning to a feature list.

### 7. Languages - 0:23-0:25.5

Preserve the strongest existing idea, but integrate it into the phone. One outgoing sentence changes naturally across Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic, French, and Japanese while the camera circles the glass. The scripts align and resolve into:

`50+ languages.`

`same problem. still handled.`

### 8. Real-world scale - 0:25.5-0:29.5

Use evidence, not confetti.

- The completed confirmations from the previous scenes multiply into a deep physical archive of receipts, bookings, claim approvals, and cancellation notices. A fast lens pull reveals that their negative space forms a monumental `20,000`.
- The same camera continues through the archive as the documents become real-looking social posts, reactions, and view surfaces. A banking move reveals `30M` through scale and perspective, not a typed counter.
- Each number gets its own clean visual landing with `in the past 30 days` attached at equal hierarchy.

### 9. Brand resolve - 0:29.5-0:32

The camera passes through `30M` and all light collapses onto the supplied real Asmi wordmark. No rotating logo, confetti, underline, or generic lens flare.

Hold:

`she doesn't stop until it's done.`

Then reveal the real iMessage and WhatsApp entry points with restrained native marks. End on one dry physical click.

## Motion system

- **Default entrance:** elements are discovered by camera focus or carried into frame by an existing object, never independently faded on.
- **Default transition:** a visible interface detail physically becomes the next interface detail.
- **Camera rule:** every move has a narrative purpose; the phone settles square whenever text must be read.
- **Depth rule:** every major shot has foreground, subject, and background separation with real parallax.
- **Restraint rule:** no bouncing, decorative orbiting, repeated shockwaves, random rotations, emoji symbols, pills, card carousels, progress rails, particle confetti, or centered stat slides.
- **Task-icon rule:** use one consistent pictogram family only where it improves instant recognition; keep icons embedded in real artifacts and subordinate to the task result.
- **Format rule:** vertical and widescreen receive separate camera framing, not one composition scaled into two ratios.

## Total soundtrack replacement

Replace the jazz-house track completely with a **modern cinematic electronic score**: urgent, tactile, premium, and grown-up.

### Instrumentation

- analog sub pulse
- dark processed piano or mallet motif
- granular synth texture
- tight electronic kick and low tom impacts
- restrained staccato strings for pursuit
- reverse-reverb and filtered-noise transitions

No walking bass, novelty brass, cartoon percussion, bright piano jabs, vocal samples, or EDM drop.

### Energy map

- **0:00-0:02.5:** near-silence, phone vibration, one low pulse
- **0:02.5-0:08:** tension begins; sparse motif and sub establish forward motion
- **0:08-0:18:** pursuit engine accelerates through four matched gear shifts, one per channel
- **0:18-0:20.5:** genuine drop in density and volume so the result lands
- **0:20.5-0:25.5:** controlled rebuild through breadth and languages
- **0:25.5-0:29.5:** two largest impacts for `20,000` and `30M`
- **0:29.5-0:32:** percussion disappears; one sustained harmonic resolution under the logo

Mix around genuine dynamic range rather than constant loudness: music approximately -16 to -18 LUFS before final mix, final master around -14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP, and clear headroom for interface foley.
