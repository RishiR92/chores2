# AI-to-AI film V6 — remove repetition, rebuild the energy

## What the audit found

The current cut repeats the same five tasks in three places:

1. as dim “CALLING…” rows behind the opening message,
2. as the outcome records in the insight scene,
3. as a second task board immediately afterward.

That repetition weakens both the suspense and the insight. The attached screenshot is the strongest and correct use of the five examples: one compact proof stack after the reveal.

The existing score was designed around a restrained suspense bed, but it stays too subdued after the reveal. The music does not create the launch-film lift, momentum, or payoff the story needs.

## Rebuild

### 1. Opening: suspense without task examples — 0:00–0:05

- Remove all five background task rows.
- Open inside one unidentified live-call environment: moving waveform fragments, call timer, cropped metadata, and a subtle forward camera push.
- Treat this as a thriller: details emerge from darkness, the frame slowly closes in, call activity feels intercepted, and each movement increases curiosity without explaining the answer.
- Keep the message focused:
  - **asmi makes thousands of calls into the real world.**
  - **lately, something changed on the other end.**
  - **listen.**
- No task names, agent labels, outcome cards, or AI-to-AI clues before the proof.

### 2. Real call: one continuous discovery — 0:05–0:16

- Preserve the clean Herman’s exchange and exact captions.
- Keep the interface visually alive with waveform behavior, timing, speaker changes, and a continuous push toward the final answer.
- Let the call feel observational and real rather than flashy: controlled waveform response, speaker focus, and mounting visual pressure as the final sentence approaches.
- Music remains rhythmic and tense beneath the voice, not sleepy or atmospheric.

### 3. Big reveal — 0:16–0:20

- “Herman’s is fully vegan” triggers a sharp visual freeze, a brief vacuum, then the call surface splits into:
  - **ASMI AGENT**
  - **BUSINESS AGENT**
- Land one unmistakable statement: **an AI just called an AI.**
- This is the first appearance of the AI-to-AI idea and the point where the full beat and color arrive.
- Make this the film’s largest motion event: the restrained camera push stops, the waveform is physically pulled apart into two speaking sides, scale and color expand beyond the frame, and the title lands with one decisive impact—not a normal scene transition.

### 4. Insight + examples in one scene only — 0:20–0:29

- Use the attached screenshot’s structure as the single home for all five examples.
- The five rows enter rapidly and accumulate beneath:
  - **more calls are being answered by agents.**
  - **agent-to-agent isn’t coming. it’s already happening.**
- Keep exactly these rows:
  - DENTIST — appointment confirmed
  - GYM — membership cancelled
  - GARDENER — visit booked
  - LANDLORD — repair chased
  - SALON — appointment moved
- Each row lands as evidence on a musical hit; no duplicates elsewhere.
- Shift from surprise to proof: animation becomes faster, rhythmic, and satisfying, with records behaving like captured evidence rather than decorative cards.

### 5. Asmi’s leadership, without another task list — 0:29–0:35

- Transform the five accumulated records into one fast, visual consumer-to-real-world flow rather than showing the tasks again.
- Show a clear directional relationship:
  - **businesses have agents answering.**
  - **consumers have asmi calling.**
- Resolve into the key conclusion:
  - **asmi is leading the consumer side of the agent-to-agent world.**
- Use the existing records as moving material so the transition feels continuous, not like a new slide.
- Shift from proof to momentum: the records compress into one fast communication flow, then widen into Asmi’s consumer-side reach. No dots, generic network diagram, or repeated task labels.

### 6. End — 0:35–0:39

- Collapse the flow into the real Asmi logo.
- Keep only: **your agent for the real world.**
- End on a confident musical payoff, not a soft fade.

## New music direction

Discard the current score completely.

Create a new 39-second instrumental with **high-energy consumer launch-film momentum**: punchy electronic drums, syncopated bass, handclap/percussive texture, a playful chopped melodic hook, and polished pop dynamics. No vocals, cinematic drones, corporate synth beds, random “tun-tun” stings, cartoon sounds, or festival EDM drop.

- **0:00–0:05:** immediate rhythmic hook; tight filtered drums and ticking percussion create curiosity from frame one.
- **0:05–0:16:** bass pulse and percussion continue under the call at lower intensity; no dead air and no loss of pace.
- **On “vegan”:** half-beat vacuum, hard impact, then full drums and hook release.
- **0:20–0:29:** five satisfying rhythmic accents synchronize with the five evidence rows.
- **0:29–0:35:** added percussion and rising bass movement drive Asmi’s leadership reveal.
- **0:35–0:39:** biggest clean lift, memorable final chord, precise logo hit.

Add restrained sound design only where it improves physicality: call connection texture, reveal snap, five paper/receipt impacts, and final logo lock.

## Scene-specific motion direction

The film will not reuse one animation style everywhere. Each section gets motion matched to its emotional job while sharing one continuous camera path:

- **Opening — thrill:** slow macro push, partial information, narrow light, restrained waveform movement, and tightening composition.
- **Call — discovery:** realistic speaker-led response, subtle pressure building, no distracting spectacle.
- **Reveal — surprise:** freeze, half-beat visual vacuum, violent split-open transformation, full color release, and the largest scale change in the film.
- **Insight — proof:** quick tactile evidence strikes synchronized to the beat; all five examples appear here once.
- **Leadership — acceleration:** the evidence itself transforms into consumer-to-real-world momentum without repeating labels.
- **End — confidence:** motion resolves into one clean logo lock and a strong final musical hit.
