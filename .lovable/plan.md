# Asmi launch film v3 - video-first rebuild

## Direction

Rebuild the 20-second film as a **flash-lit night editorial** spot inspired by the confidence and pacing of current consumer AI launch films, not by the website.

- **Background:** near-black charcoal (`#090909`) with warm off-white (`#F3F0E8`) for contrast.
- **Accent:** signal red (`#FF3B30`) only for pressure, failed attempts, and the final underline. Native iMessage blue and WhatsApp green appear only inside their real communication contexts.
- **Typography:** oversized condensed grotesque for campaign lines; neutral system typography inside phone and mail surfaces; Asmi’s italic serif wordmark only at the end.
- **Texture:** subtle flash grain, hard light sweeps, screen reflections, timecode fragments, and physical paper edges. No website grid, colorful cards, citrus/mint palette, or generic dashboard styling.
- **Motion:** fast camera pushes, match cuts through device edges, message bubbles that become email rows, and rhythmic one-frame flashes. The communication surfaces stay recognizable and readable rather than decorative.

## Story and pacing

### 0:00-0:03 - The claim
- Black frame, dry percussion starts.
- **“the most irritating assistant in the world.”** arrives in two hard beats.
- Suspense hold, then: **“for them.”**

### 0:03-0:06 - The ask
- Realistic iMessage thread, framed like an actual phone close-up:
  - You: **“they won’t let me cancel my gym. i’ve tried twice.”**
  - Asmi: **“send me your member ID.”**
  - You: **“GX-88214”**
  - Asmi: **“got it. don’t think about it again.”**
- The member ID becomes the visual thread connecting every next action.

### 0:06-0:13 - She starts chasing
Show one communication surface at a time, close enough to read:
- **Call:** native-style phone screen, Mission gym, dialling, 6-minute hold, voicemail.
- **SMS:**
  - Asmi: **“Hi - confirming cancellation for membership GX-88214, effective today. Please send written confirmation.”**
  - Gym: **“Cancellations have to be completed in person.”**
  - Asmi: **“Your agreement allows written notice. I’ve emailed it now.”**
- **Mail:** realistic inbox and opened email, not a designed card:
  - To: `cancellations@missiongym.com`
  - Subject: **Cancellation notice - GX-88214**
  - Body requests cancellation under the written-notice clause, effective today, plus written confirmation and refund of the final charge.
- **Call again:** the email collapses into the phone screen; second call connects.

All company and contact details remain fictional, while the language, timestamps, status bars, controls, and message behavior look authentic.

### 0:13-0:16 - Works across languages
- The final sentence from the email travels across realistic message bubbles in Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic, French, and Japanese.
- Scripts move on the beat and resolve into **“50+ languages.”**
- Keep this as a visual translation capability moment, not a field of floating labels.

### 0:16-0:18 - Proof
- A credible confirmation email lands:
  - **“Your membership has been cancelled.”**
  - **“$59 refunded.”**
  - **Confirmation GX-88214**
- The percussion drops out for the confirmation sound and one satisfying impact.

### 0:18-0:20 - Final line
- Hard cut to the Asmi wordmark.
- End on the requested line, large and uninterrupted:
  - **“she doesn’t stop until it’s done.”**
- Small final destination line: **“text asmi.”** with iMessage and WhatsApp marks.

## Music and sound

- Replace the current score with a **vocal-free percussive club track** around 128-134 BPM: tight kick, dry clap, restrained bass, no glossy corporate synths.
- Edit picture to the music rather than laying music underneath afterward:
  - sparse opening pulse for suspense
  - faster subdivisions through call, text, and mail
  - rising pressure on the second call
  - half-beat silence before cancellation confirmation
  - final bass hit under **“done.”**
- Use only restrained, believable interface sounds: send, voicemail click, mail send, call connect, confirmation. No call recordings.
