# Asmi film v5: make the escalation unmistakable

## Story order

1. **Introduce Asmi** — keep the crisp three-beat opening: “the most irritating” → “assistant.” → “for them.”
2. **Explain the behavior first** — before any example, show one clear kinetic sentence: “she tries every way in.” A visual relay makes **call → text → email → call again** readable in one glance, then lands on **“until it’s done.”**
3. **Show what people hand her** — a fast, dynamic task field built from the website’s real examples: **cancel a subscription, chase an insurance claim, reschedule a flight, book a dentist, get a plumber, fight a charge, handle a ghosting landlord, renew a passport, find a vet slot, make a dinner reservation.** The requests arrive like real messages, not feature cards, and collapse into one chosen task.
4. **Show the gym example** — open on the realistic iMessage request, then move through four clearly numbered, full-screen beats: first call reaches voicemail, text meets resistance, email cites the agreement, second call connects. Keep the member ID pinned across every surface so viewers understand it is one continuing task.
5. **Show the result** — hold the cancellation confirmation long enough to read: membership cancelled, $59 refunded, member ID matched.
6. **Reveal languages** — preserve the existing multilingual message transformation and “50+ languages.” lockup after the proof.
7. **Show real-world momentum** — after languages, turn completed task fragments into a fast, cinematic proof sequence: **“20,000 real-world tasks completed”** and **“30 million people checked out Asmi on socials”**, unified by one clear qualifier: **“in the past 30 days.”** Give each number its own beat so neither feels like fine print or a generic stats card.
8. **Finish with the real brand** — let the “past 30 days” motion collapse into the supplied Asmi logo, then hold “she doesn’t stop until it’s done.” and the iMessage/WhatsApp invitation.

## Motion and clarity

- Use the recurring chase pulse as a literal handoff between channels, with each completed action activating the next icon and surface.
- Replace ambiguous step labels with an always-visible four-node progress rail: **call / text / email / call again**. Completed nodes stay marked while the current node pulses.
- Give each phone surface a clean readable landing before it moves; no overlapping transitions or generic wipes.
- Keep the distinctive physical-phone camera movement, but reduce rotation while text is on screen.
- Make the task-range beat feel generated live: incoming requests rapidly reshape the same visual canvas, then the gym request interrupts and takes over the frame.
- Present the achievement figures as kinetic evidence, not dashboard counters: the task fragments accumulate into **20,000**, then multiply into a social-view field that resolves to **30 million**.

## Timing and sound

- Target roughly **26–28 seconds** so the channel explanation, task range, gym escalation, outcome, languages, achievement figures, and final logo each remain readable.
- Recut the club track around the new structure using the existing percussion only; no melodic “tun tun tun” cue.
- Use restrained send, connection, and confirmation sounds to reinforce channel handoffs without call recordings.
