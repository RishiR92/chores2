# Asmi AI-to-AI film — fresh concept rebuild

## Creative reset

Replace the current film rather than polishing its existing structure. This version will not reuse the paper grid, stamps, Bricolage/mono pairing, paired phone cards, cable graphic, bright scene-by-scene color swaps, old score, or outcome-tile montage.

The new idea is **“One call inside thousands.”** Start with the scale of Asmi operating across the real world, then dive into one uninterrupted AI-to-AI restaurant call. The audience experiences the insight as it happens: an AI answers Asmi, understands the request, gives useful information, and moves the task forward.

Primary master remains 1920×1080 for LinkedIn/X. The 1080×1920 version will be recomposed independently.

## Story and exact copy

### 1. Scale with impact — 0:00–0:05

Open in near-darkness on hundreds of small, living call traces spread across a dimensional field. Each trace resolves into a tiny point of light; the field rapidly expands until it feels larger than the frame.

Copy appears in only two beats:

- `thousands of people use asmi`
- `to deal with the real world.`

The camera selects one active signal and dives into it. No badges, lists, or supporting paragraphs.

### 2. The new encounter — 0:05–0:11

The selected signal becomes one continuous call space: two distinct voice presences sharing the same visual field, not two phone mockups.

Use the clean Herman’s response beginning after the interrupted opening:

- `“Yes, of course. I can help you with a booking, cancellation, or any questions about Herman’s.”`
- `“What can I do for John today?”`

Minimal reveal copy:

- `the voice that answered?`
- `also AI.`

The second line lands only after the answering voice is clearly heard.

### 3. One continuous real conversation — 0:11–0:28

Stay with the same call and preserve conversational order. Use clean, complete sections only:

- Asmi: `“John is looking to visit on August 21st and wanted to check what vegetarian menu options you’ll have available that day.”`
- Herman’s: `“Herman’s is fully vegan, so every dish on the buffet is plant-based.”`
- Herman’s: `“The buffet rotates daily, so I can’t promise which exact dishes will be on the menu that day.”`

Visual information should form directly from the exchange: the date locks into place, a menu field becomes fully plant-based, and the rotating selection remains visibly variable. The camera never resets, and nothing appears unless the spoken line causes it.

No second gardening example. One well-told call is stronger and avoids the fragmented compilation feeling.

### 4. The insight — 0:28–0:34

Pull back from the single conversation and reveal that it is one of many active AI-to-AI encounters in the larger field.

Copy:

- `AI is starting to answer the phone.`
- `asmi already knows how to talk to it.`

Keep each line on screen separately. No explanatory subtitle underneath.

### 5. Iconic resolve — 0:34–0:38

All activity compresses into the real Asmi wordmark on a clean field.

Final copy, one line only:

- `built for whoever answers.`

No CTA, channel icons, extra tagline, or stacked closing claims. Hold the final mark long enough to register.

## New visual language

- **World:** deep graphite space, soft warm-white type, one electric signal green, and a restrained amber accent. No reused Asmi-film palette or paper texture.
- **Typography:** a new contemporary grotesk with a calm editorial cut; one family, two weights. Large type, generous negative space, no rotated labels or stamp treatment.
- **Core motif:** volumetric call traces that behave like living routes. Speech creates pulses, useful facts condense into precise objects, and the camera follows one signal through the whole film.
- **Motion:** continuous camera choreography with depth, focus pulls, and match movement. Avoid slides, wipes, bouncing cards, mirrored devices, dashboards, waveforms, and decorative effects.
- **Reality:** identify speakers through restrained spatial labels and exact captions. Do not simulate a fake live transcript or show generic robot imagery.

## Audio edit and score

- Rebuild from the source Herman’s recording, not the existing stitched excerpt.
- Remove the overlapping/cut-off 0:00–0:10 opening entirely.
- Cut only at detected silence boundaries, preserving small handles and complete sentence tails.
- Keep the chosen dialogue in original chronological order so it plays as one conversation, even where dead air is tightened.
- Hard-stop or naturally decay each spoken excerpt before any insight or end frame begins; no dialogue may spill into the following beat.
- Add frame-accurate caption timing from the final edited waveform, not from approximate transcript blocks.
- Replace the previous score with a new premium launch cue: tense minimal pulse at the start, controlled momentum under dialogue, then a single widening lift for the insight. No random beat drops, playful “tun-tun” motif, cartoon sounds, or constant full-energy bed.
- Duck music under every spoken line, clean the call conservatively, and master for phone speakers at about -14 LUFS with true peak no higher than -1 dBTP.
