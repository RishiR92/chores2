## Visual and motion direction

- **Mood:** international consumer-tech film with Paris-night polish—sleek, tactile, warm, and fast without becoming cartoonish.
- **Interface realism:** faithful iMessage, Phone, and Mail surfaces; correct iPhone proportions; no generic chat bubbles or symbolic fake controls.
- **Continuous motion:** every transition is caused by the previous action—message becomes work receipt, call status becomes Phone, waveform becomes email text, send motion becomes delivery confirmation.
- **Dynamic pacing:** quick tactile moves during search and confirmations; slower readable holds during dialogue; strongest acceleration into the language reveal.
- **Clean hierarchy:** remove decorative elements behind primary copy. Texture and motion move to the edges whenever text is on screen.
- Compose widescreen and vertical independently so the phone remains tall and readable in both.
