# asmi 18-second launch film

Create two purpose-built edits from one story and audio system:
- **Vertical:** 1080 × 1920 for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and mobile sharing
- **Widescreen:** 1920 × 1080 for the website, decks, and launch screens
- **Length:** 18 seconds at 30fps
- **Audio:** hype instrumental, real call snippets, message pops, and crisp interface sound design; no narrator

## Creative direction: “polite chaos, handled”

The site comes alive at launch-trailer speed: bold paper-and-ink graphics, oversized lowercase copy, chat bubbles that transform into task interfaces, rapid call states, and satisfying completion receipts. The energy is young, witty, tactile, and unmistakably asmi—not a generic AI promo.

**Palette:** paper `#FBF7F0`, ink `#141318`, electric blue `#2F5BFF`, coral `#FF5A47`, citrus `#E8FF5A`, mint `#35D6A4`.

**Typography:** Bricolage Grotesque / Archivo Black for blunt statements, Instrument Serif italic for the asmi wordmark, Space Mono for live statuses.

**Motion language:** masked rises, quick overshooting springs, chat-bubble expansion, paper wipes, marker swipes, live timers, and status pulses. No fade-to-black and no static slideshow frames.

## Shot-by-shot plan

### 0:00–0:02 — Hook
Fragments of “on hold,” “voicemail,” and “no slots” pile up for a split second, then the asmi wordmark shoves them away.

**Copy:**
> the most irritating assistant in the world.

Smaller stamp:
> annoying to them. invisible to you.

**Sound:** Music hits immediately, telephone click, message pop.

### 0:02–0:06 — Plan visually
An iMessage request lands:
> dinner for 5. actual veg food.

The bubble expands into three image-led restaurant options from the website. Ratings and distance snap in; Wayfare Tavern is selected.

**Copy:**
> she plans it with you.

This proves visual discovery without explaining “generative UI.”

### 0:06–0:10 — Then act
The selected restaurant card compresses into a live call state. Show only a timer, waveform, and unmistakable statuses—never a fake transcript.

**Statuses:**
- calling now
- on hold
- booked. 7:30.

**Copy:**
> then she gets it done.

**Sound:** Use one short, highly intelligible real call excerpt. Music ducks to near-minimum so both sides of the call are equally clear.

### 0:10–0:14 — Chase and breadth
One request branches into rapid channel updates:

> call → voicemail → email → call again

Three task receipts punch in around it:
- dentist, tues 8:30
- HVAC, saturday 9am
- $60 back

The Spanish grandpa call flashes briefly as a fourth proof point, followed by a dense language cluster compressing into:

> 50+ languages.

**Copy:**
> whatever it takes is kind of the point.

### 0:14–0:18 — Payoff
All receipts and language labels pull inward to form the asmi wordmark. A coral marker line draws underneath. iMessage and WhatsApp icons settle below.

**Closing copy:**
> she doesn't stop until it's done.

Final beat:
> text asmi.

**Sound:** Completion hit, message pop, and a decisive musical ending rather than a vague fade.

## Audio treatment

- Keep the existing original doctor, Marco HVAC, and Spanish grandpa recordings available, but use only the strongest one or two micro-excerpts so the edit stays readable.
- Normalize each speaker separately so the user and asmi voices have matching perceived loudness.
- Duck music aggressively and smoothly under every spoken moment; dialogue must remain dominant on a phone speaker.
- Use a punchy, vocal-free track with an immediate hook, a small lift around 10 seconds, and a clean 18-second ending.
- Keep sound effects sparse: one incoming message, tactile selection ticks, one call connection cue, and one completion hit.

## Format adaptation

The two edits share exact timing, copy, and audio but are composed independently.

**Vertical:** full-frame phone moments, one dominant idea per beat, stacked receipts, large safe-area typography.

**Widescreen:** asymmetric editorial framing, product motion on one side and kinetic copy on the other, horizontal task branching.
